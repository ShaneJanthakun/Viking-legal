Privacy Policy

Viking: Party Game — Last updated: 7 August 2026

Summary

Viking: Party Game does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads.

Data stored on your device

Player names, custom decks, and photos you add to custom cards are stored only locally on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. To validate purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and purchase receipts. This identifier cannot be used to identify you personally. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Photos

If you add photos to custom cards, the app asks for photo library access. Selected photos never leave your device.

Children

The app is rated 16+ and is not directed at children.
